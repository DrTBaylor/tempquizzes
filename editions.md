# Editions of Sorry!

Sorry! has been reprinted many times since the 1930s. This is an overview of the main types of edition. Details and dates vary by country and printing, so check a specific box if you need exact information.

## Early editions (1930s–1950s)

- The first UK editions came from **Waddingtons**; US editions from **Parker Brothers** starting in 1934.
- Early sets often had **wooden pawns** and simple artwork.

## Classic Parker Brothers era (1960s–1980s)

- Plastic pawns became standard.
- The familiar square board with four colored sides, slides, and Safety Zones settled into the design most people know.

## Hasbro era (1990s onward)

- After Hasbro acquired Parker Brothers' parent company in 1991, the game kept the Parker Brothers brand name for many years.
- Hasbro has refreshed the artwork and packaging several times.

## Modern rule changes

Some editions since about **2013** made changes such as:
- each player using **3 pawns** instead of 4,
- one pawn starting already on the board,
- the **Sorry!** card offering an alternative: move a pawn forward 4 spaces instead of bumping,
- the **Fire and Ice** edition, which adds power-up tokens that speed pawns up or freeze them.

## Special and collector's editions

- **Retro / vintage editions** that copy older artwork and wooden pieces.
- **Wooden and deluxe editions** with larger boards and storage drawers.
- **Themed editions** using characters from films, TV and other brands.
- **Travel editions** with a smaller board.
