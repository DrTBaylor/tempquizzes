# Variants and Spin-offs

## Official variations in the classic rules

### Team play (4 players)
Partners sit across from each other (for example, red and yellow vs. blue and green). On your turn you may move your own pawn **or** your partner's. A team wins when all eight of its pawns are Home. You may not bump or swap your partner's pawns if another move is possible.

### Hand-of-cards variant
Each player is dealt **5 cards**. On your turn, play one card from your hand, then draw a replacement. This adds planning, since you can save cards such as the Sorry! or the 11 for the best moment.

## Popular house rules

- **Any card to start:** let pawns leave Start on any card number, for faster games.
- **Extra 2 draws only if you moved:** a 2 gives another turn only when you used it.
- **Sorry! bonus:** draw again after playing a Sorry! card.
- **Pawns start on the board:** begin with one pawn already outside Start.
- **No forced moves:** you may skip a turn instead of making a move that hurts you.

Agree on house rules before the game begins.

## Spin-off games

Hasbro has released several games under the Sorry! name, including:

- **Sorry! Sliders:** a dexterity game where players slide pawns down a ramp at a target board, a bit like shuffleboard.
- **Sorry! Revenge:** a card game version.
- **Sorry! Express:** a quicker version played with dice.
- **Sorry! Spin:** a version with a spinning center that changes the board during play.
- Video game versions for PC, consoles and mobile.

Exact release years and contents vary by country and printing.
