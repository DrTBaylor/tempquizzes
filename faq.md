# Sorry! FAQ

**Can I leave Start with any card?**
No. In the classic rules only a **1** or **2** moves a pawn out of Start. A **Sorry!** card also brings a pawn out, by moving it onto an opponent's space.

**Do I have to move if I don't want to?**
Yes. If any legal move exists, you must make it, even if it's bad for you. The one exception is the **11** swap, which is optional.

**What if no move is possible?**
You lose your turn. Discard the card.

**Do I still draw again on a 2 if I couldn't move?**
Yes, in the classic rules a 2 always gives another draw.

**Can I land on my own pawn?**
No. If the only move would put a pawn on a space held by your own pawn, you can't make it.

**Can I move backward into my Safety Zone?**
No, you can only enter the Safety Zone moving forward. But you *can* move backward out of it with a 4 or a 10.

**Can I move backward past my Start exit?**
Yes. Moving backward from near your Start exit is a common shortcut toward your Safety Zone.

**Do I need an exact count to get Home?**
Yes. A move that would overshoot Home can't be made.

**Can pawns in the Safety Zone be bumped, swapped or hit with Sorry!?**
No. They're safe from all opponent actions.

**Can I split a 7 to bring a pawn out of Start?**
No. Both parts of a split 7 must be forward moves by pawns already on the board.

**Can I split a 7 and use part of it to get a pawn Home?**
Yes, as long as that part takes the pawn exactly Home.

**Does sliding bump my own pawns?**
Yes. Every pawn on the slide goes back to its Start, including yours.

**What happens on a slide of my own color?**
Nothing. You stay on the triangle.

**Can I swap with a pawn in Start or Home using an 11?**
No. Swaps only work with opponent pawns on the main track.
