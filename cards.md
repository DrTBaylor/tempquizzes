# The Sorry! Cards

The classic deck has **45 cards**: five **1** cards, four each of **2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12**, and four **Sorry!** cards. There are **no 6 or 9 cards**.

| Card | Count | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 5 | Move a pawn out of Start, **or** move a pawn forward 1 space. |
| **2** | 4 | Move a pawn out of Start, **or** move a pawn forward 2 spaces. Either way, **draw again** and take another turn. |
| **3** | 4 | Move a pawn forward 3 spaces. |
| **4** | 4 | Move a pawn **backward** 4 spaces. |
| **5** | 4 | Move a pawn forward 5 spaces. |
| **7** | 4 | Move one pawn forward 7 spaces, **or split** the 7 between two pawns (for example, 4 and 3). You can't use part of a 7 to leave Start. |
| **8** | 4 | Move a pawn forward 8 spaces. |
| **10** | 4 | Move a pawn forward 10 spaces, **or** backward 1 space. |
| **11** | 4 | Move a pawn forward 11 spaces, **or swap** places with any opponent's pawn on the main track. The swap is optional: if you can't (or don't want to) move 11, you may forfeit the turn. |
| **12** | 4 | Move a pawn forward 12 spaces. |
| **Sorry!** | 4 | Take a pawn from your Start and put it on any space occupied by an opponent's pawn on the main track, bumping that pawn back to its Start. If you have no pawn in Start or no opponent is on the track, you lose your turn. |

## Notes

- **2 is the only card that gives an extra draw** in the classic rules, even if you couldn't move.
- The **Sorry!** card and the **11** swap can't target pawns in a Safety Zone or Home.
- Some newer editions let a Sorry! card instead move a pawn forward 4 spaces. See [editions.md](editions.md).
