# Sorry! Strategy

Sorry! has a lot of luck, but good choices still win games. Here are the key ideas.

## 1. Use the backward shortcut

Your Safety Zone entrance is just behind your Start exit. If a pawn has just left Start, a **4** (backward 4) or **10** (backward 1) can put it a few spaces from your Safety Zone, skipping almost a full lap. Keep a pawn near your Start exit so you can use backward cards this way.

## 2. Get pawns out early

You can only leave Start with a 1, 2 or Sorry! card. With several pawns on the board you have more choices for each card and are less likely to waste a turn.

## 3. Split your 7s wisely

A 7 can be split between two pawns. Use it to:
- bring a pawn into Home by exact count while moving another pawn too,
- bump two opponents at once,
- move a pawn out of danger while advancing another.

## 4. Use the 11 swap as a weapon

Swapping with an opponent can:
- jump you far ahead by trading places with the leading opponent,
- send an opponent who's about to reach their Safety Zone back behind you.

Remember the swap is optional, so don't make a swap that sets you back.

## 5. Save the Sorry! for the right moment

You can't choose when you draw it, but when you have one, aim at the opponent pawn that is **furthest along**, ideally one about to enter its Safety Zone.

## 6. Watch the slides

Landing on another color's slide moves you forward and can bump several pawns at once. Avoid leaving your pawns sitting on slides where opponents can land on the triangle.

## 7. Stay safe

- Pawns in the Safety Zone can't be touched, so get them there.
- Avoid parking pawns right in front of opponents, especially 1–12 spaces ahead, where many cards can hit you.
- A pawn just past an opponent's Start exit is at risk from pawns entering the board.

## 8. Think about the endgame

Getting Home needs exact counts. Keep pawns at several distances from Home so that more cards are useful to you.
