      Player Stats

      How the computer players choose: for every legal move on the card they drew, they score the resulting board (their own progress, minus the leading opponent's, with a penalty for pawns sitting 1–12 spaces in front of an opponent) and pick the best. Moves follow the classic rules in rules.md: leave Start on a 1 or 2, exact count into Home, 7s can be split, 11s can swap, 2s draw again, and slides bump every pawn on them.
