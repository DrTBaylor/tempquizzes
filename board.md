# The Sorry! Board

The board is a square track with one color assigned to each side: **red, blue, yellow and green**.

## Main track

- The outer track has **60 spaces**, 15 on each side.
- All pawns share this track and move clockwise around it.

## Start

- Each color has a round **Start** area beside the track.
- Pawns enter the track on the space just outside Start, using a 1, 2 or Sorry! card.

## Safety Zone

- Each color has a **Safety Zone**: a path of 5 colored spaces branching off the main track toward the center.
- The entrance sits a short way *before* your own Start exit, so a pawn travels almost the full lap before turning in.
- Only pawns of that color may enter. Pawns inside are safe from bumping and swapping.

## Home

- At the end of each Safety Zone is the round **Home** area.
- Pawns must land in Home by exact count. Once Home, a pawn is finished and can't be moved.

## Slides

- Each side has **two slides**, one shorter and one longer, marked with a triangle at the start and a circle at the end.
- Landing exactly on the triangle of a slide in another player's color sends your pawn to the end of the slide, bumping every pawn on it back to Start (your own included).
- Slides of your own color do nothing for you.

## Layout tip

Because your Safety Zone entrance is just *behind* your Start exit, a pawn that leaves Start and then moves **backward** (with a 4 or a 10) can land close to the Safety Zone, skipping almost the whole lap. See [strategy.md](strategy.md).
