# Sorry! Trivia

- **The name is the game.** The title comes from what you say (often not very sincerely) when you bump an opponent back to Start.
- **No 6s or 9s.** The deck skips these numbers, which also avoids mixing up a 6 and a 9 when a card is upside down.
- **The 1 card is the most common.** There are five 1 cards and four of each other card.
- **It's nearly 100 years old.** Sorry! was patented in England in 1929 and came to the US in 1934.
- **Cards replaced dice.** Its biggest change from Pachisi, Ludo and Parcheesi was swapping dice for a deck of cards.
- **Going backward can be the fastest way home.** Thanks to the board layout, a backward 4 from near your Start can save almost a whole lap.
- **A family of race games.** Sorry! is a cousin of Ludo (UK), Parcheesi (US), Mensch ärgere Dich nicht (Germany) and Trouble (US), all descended from Pachisi.
- **Long-running Hasbro classic.** It has stayed in print for decades alongside games like Monopoly and Clue.
