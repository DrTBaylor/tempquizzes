# Sorry! Glossary

**Bump** — Sending an opponent's pawn back to its Start by landing on its space, sliding over it, or playing a Sorry! card.

**Discard pile** — Where played cards go. It's reshuffled into a new deck when the draw pile runs out.

**Exact count** — The rule that a pawn must land precisely in Home; overshooting isn't allowed.

**Forfeit** — Losing your turn because no legal move exists (or by choosing not to use an 11).

**Home** — The round area at the end of each Safety Zone. A pawn in Home is finished.

**Main track** — The 60-space outer path shared by all players.

**Pawn** — A playing piece. Each player has four in the classic game.

**Safety Zone** — A 5-space colored path leading from the main track to Home. Only the matching color can enter, and pawns there can't be bumped or swapped.

**Slide** — A marked stretch of the track. Landing on the triangle at the start of another color's slide moves your pawn to its end and bumps everything on it.

**Sorry! card** — A card that lets you move a pawn from Start onto an opponent's space, bumping that pawn back to its Start.

**Split** — Dividing a 7 card's movement between two pawns.

**Start** — The round area where each player's pawns begin and where bumped pawns return.

**Swap** — Trading places with an opponent's pawn using an 11 card.
