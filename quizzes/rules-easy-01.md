# Quiz: Sorry! Basics

- **Topics:** rules.md, cards.md
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Questions:** 10

---

**1.** How many pawns does each player have in the classic game?

- A) 2
- B) 3
- C) 4
- D) 5

**2.** Which cards let a pawn leave Start in the classic rules (not counting the Sorry! card)?

- A) 1 and 2
- B) 1 and 6
- C) 2 and 12
- D) Any card

**3.** True or False: The 4 card moves a pawn backward.

**4.** How many cards are in the classic Sorry! deck?

**5.** Which card lets you split its move between two pawns?

- A) 5
- B) 7
- C) 10
- D) 12

**6.** True or False: Pawns in the Safety Zone can be bumped by opponents.

**7.** What happens when you draw a 2?

- A) You move backward 2
- B) You lose your turn
- C) You move and then draw again
- D) You swap places with an opponent

**8.** Which two numbers are missing from the deck?

**9.** True or False: A pawn must reach Home by exact count.

**10.** What does the 11 card let you do instead of moving forward 11?

- A) Move backward 11
- B) Swap places with an opponent's pawn
- C) Take a pawn out of Start
- D) Draw two more cards

---

## Answer Key

1. **C** — Each player has four pawns. *(source: rules.md)*
2. **A** — Only a 1 or a 2 moves a pawn out of Start. *(source: rules.md)*
3. **True** — The 4 moves a pawn backward 4 spaces. *(source: cards.md)*
4. **45** — Five 1s, four each of nine other numbers, and four Sorry! cards. *(source: cards.md)*
5. **B** — A 7 can be split between two pawns. *(source: cards.md)*
6. **False** — Pawns in the Safety Zone are safe from bumping and swapping. *(source: rules.md)*
7. **C** — The 2 is the only card that gives an extra draw. *(source: cards.md)*
8. **6 and 9** — The deck has no 6 or 9 cards. *(source: cards.md)*
9. **True** — Overshooting Home isn't allowed. *(source: rules.md)*
10. **B** — The 11 can swap places with an opponent's pawn on the main track. *(source: cards.md)*
