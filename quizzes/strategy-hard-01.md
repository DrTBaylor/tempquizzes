# Quiz: Sorry! Strategy Challenge

- **Topics:** strategy.md
- **Difficulty:** hard
- **Questions:** 10

---

**1.** Your pawn has just left Start and is sitting on the space outside your Start circle. You draw a **4**. According to strategy.md, why is this often a great draw?

- A) It lets you bring a second pawn out of Start
- B) Moving backward 4 can put the pawn close to your Safety Zone, skipping almost a full lap
- C) It lets you swap places with the leading opponent
- D) It gives you an extra draw

**2.** Name the **two** cards that can be used for the backward shortcut toward your Safety Zone.

**3.** You have one pawn on the board and three still in Start. Why does strategy.md recommend getting more pawns out early?

- A) Pawns in Start can be bumped by a Sorry! card
- B) Pawns out of Start count toward your score
- C) With more pawns on the board you have more choices for each card and are less likely to waste a turn
- D) You must have all pawns out before any can enter Home

**4.** One of your pawns is exactly **3** spaces from Home, and another is on the main track with open space ahead. You draw a **7**. What's the strongest play?

- A) Move the second pawn forward 7
- B) Move the first pawn 3 into Home and the second pawn forward 4
- C) Move the first pawn forward 7
- D) Forfeit the turn to keep your options open

**5.** True or False: You draw an **11**, and every possible swap would leave you further behind. Because you can make a swap, you must make one.

**6.** You draw a **Sorry!** card. Opponents have these pawns on the main track:
- Blue: a pawn that just left its Start
- Yellow: a pawn in the middle of the board
- Green: a pawn about to enter its Safety Zone

Which pawn does strategy.md suggest you bump?

**7.** Your only pawn on the board is far behind, and Red's leading pawn is about to reach its Safety Zone. You draw an **11**. Why is the swap a strong choice here?

- A) It moves your pawn straight into your own Safety Zone
- B) It jumps your pawn far ahead and sends Red's pawn back behind you
- C) It bumps Red's pawn back to its Start
- D) It lets you draw again

**8.** True or False: Leaving your pawn on another player's slide is safe, because only the pawn that lands on the triangle is affected.

**9.** Near the end of the game you have three pawns left. Which setup gives you the best chance of using each card you draw?

- A) All three pawns 2 spaces from Home
- B) Pawns 2, 5 and 8 spaces from Home
- C) All three pawns in Start
- D) All three pawns side by side on the main track

**10.** Apart from spaces 1–12 in front of an opponent, strategy.md names one more risky spot to leave a pawn. Where is it, and why is it risky?

---

## Answer Key

1. **B** — Your Safety Zone entrance is just behind your Start exit, so backing up can skip almost a full lap. *(source: strategy.md)*
2. **4 and 10** — The 4 moves backward 4 and the 10 can move backward 1. *(source: strategy.md)*
3. **C** — More pawns on the board means more options and fewer wasted turns. *(source: strategy.md)*
4. **B** — Splitting the 7 lets you reach Home by exact count while still moving another pawn. *(source: strategy.md)*
5. **False** — The 11 swap is optional, so don't make one that sets you back. *(source: strategy.md)*
6. **Green** — Aim the Sorry! at the pawn furthest along, ideally one about to enter its Safety Zone. *(source: strategy.md)*
7. **B** — Swapping with the leader moves you far ahead and sends a nearly-safe opponent back. *(source: strategy.md)*
8. **False** — Avoid leaving pawns on slides where opponents can land on the triangle; a slide can bump several pawns at once. *(source: strategy.md)*
9. **B** — Getting Home needs exact counts, so pawns at different distances make more cards useful. *(source: strategy.md)*
10. **Just past an opponent's Start exit** — Pawns entering the board from that Start can land on it. *(source: strategy.md)*
