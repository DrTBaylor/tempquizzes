# Classic Rules of Sorry!

## Object

Be the first player to move all four of your pawns from your **Start** area, around the board, and into your **Home**.

## Equipment

- Game board
- 16 pawns (4 each in red, blue, yellow and green)
- 45-card deck (see [cards.md](cards.md))

## Setup

1. Each player chooses a color and places their four pawns in the matching Start circle.
2. Shuffle the deck and place it face down.
3. The youngest player usually goes first; play continues clockwise.

## Taking a turn

1. Draw the top card.
2. Move one of your pawns according to the card (some cards let you choose between options).
3. Place the card on the discard pile. When the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discards to make a new deck.

## Leaving Start

A pawn can leave Start **only** with a **1** or a **2** card (or by using a **Sorry!** card). It moves to the space just outside your Start circle.

## Moving

- Pawns move **clockwise** unless a card says to move backward (the **4** and the **10**).
- You may jump over any pawn, including your own, while counting spaces.
- Two pawns can never share a space.

## Bumping

If your pawn ends its move on a space occupied by an **opponent's** pawn, that pawn is "bumped" back to its own Start. If the space is occupied by **your own** pawn, the move is not allowed, and you must choose a different move or lose your turn.

## Slides

Each side of the board has two slides. If your pawn lands exactly on the triangle at the start of a slide that is **not your color**, it slides to the end. Any pawns on the slide — including your own — are bumped back to Start. If the slide is your color, you stay on the triangle and do not slide.

## Safety Zone and Home

- Near your Start is your colored **Safety Zone**, a 5-space path leading to Home. Only you may enter it.
- Pawns in the Safety Zone cannot be bumped or swapped by opponents.
- A pawn must reach Home by **exact count**. If a card would take it past Home, that move can't be made.
- Backward moves (4 or 10) can move a pawn back out of the Safety Zone.

## Forced moves

If you can make a move, you must, even if it hurts you. The exception is the **11** card's swap, which is optional. If no move is possible, you lose your turn.

## Winning

The first player to get all four pawns Home wins.
